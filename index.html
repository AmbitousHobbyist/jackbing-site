Who I Am

Here for a
good time.

I'm Jack. I run Wildflower (cafe + streetwear), make daily content across YouTube and TikTok, and have spent 10+ years in digital marketing, media, design, and video editing. Why? It comes down to three things: I love creating, learning, and entertaining. I always find a way to be doing (at least) one of those things!

Whether it's a new piece of tech, a gaming playthrough, a retro mod, or my Nissan 300ZX project car, I want to bring you along for the ride of me trying new things, failing a lot, and learning a whole heap along the way! One of my favourite quotes is "Failure is not an option, its a necessity"

I want to encourage people to follow their passions and not be afraid to dive in and learn something new. That's the whole point of the channel, give it a go, have fun, figure it out.

As you've seen above this, the numbers back it up too, and many years of brand partnerships with companies like TeuTeuf, Fanatee, and Trainwreck Labs. Brands I work with get authentic integration in front of an audience that actually pays attention. Crazy!
